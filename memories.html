🎮 The Beginning

Jab Hum Game Mein Mile

Woh din yaad hai? Ek game ki lobby mein achanak tumhari entry hui — aur pata hi nahi chala kab tum mere favourite ban gaye. Kisi ne socha bhi nahi tha ki ek game itni khoobsurat dosti dega. 🥹

😂 Funny Times

Woh Haste Huye Raatein

Raat ko 2 baje tak baatein karna, funny moments share karna, ek dusre ko roast karna — aur phir bhi has has ke pet dukhna. Tumhari woh hasi yaad hai jo kabhi bhool nahi sakta. 😂💛

🎂 Birthday Special

Tumhara Birthday Celebrate Hua

Aur woh din aaya — tumhara birthday! Maine socha tha kuch special karna hai. Pata nahi kaise hua par tumhe surprise mila aur tumhari woh smile... uss smile ne sab kuch worth it bana diya. 🎂✨ Tumhe dekh ke laga ki kuch logon ki khushi hi sabse bada tohfa hota hai. 🥹

🌙 Deep Talks

Raat Ki Gehri Baatein

Jab duniya so rahi hoti thi, hum jaag ke baatein karte the. Tumne mujhe sikhaya ki dard baatne se kam hota hai, aur dosti sirf waqt nahi, dil se banti hai. Tum mere liye sirf dost nahi ho — tum ek lesson ho zindagi ka. 🌙

🥹 Emotions

Phool Tumhari Care Yaad Hai

Jab main down tha, Phool tumne ek word mein sab theek kar diya. Jab maine galti ki, Phool tumne daanta par saath bhi diya. Phool tumhari woh care, woh concern — yeh kisi kitab mein nahi padha. Phool tum sach mein ek teacher ho — life ke asli teacher. 🙏

✨ Forever

Thank You Phool

Aaj Teacher's Day hai aur main kehna chahta hoon — Phool tum best ho, yaad hai na woh din jo hum saath bitaye? Har din ek lesson tha, har moment ek gift tha. Thank you for being my teacher, my friend, my everything. 💛🙏
